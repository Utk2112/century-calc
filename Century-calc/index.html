ABOUT THIS TOOL

A quick way to understand centuries.

CenturyCalc is a small educational tool designed to make century calculations quick and easy.

A century contains 100 years. For example, years 1–100 belong to the 1st century, years 101–200 belong to the 2nd century, and years 2001–2100 belong to the 21st century.

Formula Century = ⌊(Year − 1) / 100⌋ + 1
